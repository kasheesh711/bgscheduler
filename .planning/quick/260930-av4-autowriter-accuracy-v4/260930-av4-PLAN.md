# Autowriter v4: who-did-what and homework rules, stricter judge

Scope copied from the approved plan. Real student names from the 29 Sep posts are replaced by "student A",
"student B" and "another student" here, and the nickname example uses Tim / Timothy (no real student names in the
repo).

## Hard rules
- No writes to Wise, no writes to the production database (tests use Testcontainers and fakes), no migrations.
- Do not touch the write modes of `scripts/autowrite-online-feedback.ts`.
- Repo conventions (CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md): tests in `__tests__`, double quotes, semicolons, JSDoc on exported
  functions, design-decision comments.
- Open a DRAFT PR against `feat/autowriter-loop-phase0`. Never merge, never enable auto-merge, never mark ready.

## Why (root causes found on 29 Sep posts)
1. Student A: Wise's AI summary said "[TUTOR] expressed concerns about incomplete exam preparation, noting that
   <another student> mentioned only 8 pages when there were 10 pages total." `redactForModel` (prompt.ts ~:54-80,
   similarity.ts `redactKnownNames`) replaces only the student's and the tutor's names, so the other student's name was
   the only real name left. The writer took that person to be the student ("Exam preparation is not yet finished, as
   <student A> said 8 of the 10 pages have been covered") and invented a homework line. The GLM judge (medium effort,
   ~100 reasoning tokens) passed it; judge.ts ~:40 says "Names are replaced by [STUDENT_1] and [TUTOR]; that is
   expected".
2. Student B: Wise's summary itself ended "…had three remaining homework problems to complete" (a mis-hearing). The
   writer followed rule 7 ("homework the summary says was set") and repeated it in the improvement field too.

## Scope (`src/lib/feedback-autowriter/`)
1. `prompt.ts` (PROMPT_VERSION 3 → 4), both evidence modes; rules 1–5 and 8–10 wording unchanged (tests pin
   substrings):
   - Rule 6 → "6. improvement: the specific weak areas and two or three concrete next steps or strategies to practise
     before the next lesson, written as suggestions — never as homework the tutor set, and never repeating the
     homework."
   - Rule 7 → "7. homework: only work ${record} shows the tutor clearly setting [STUDENT_1] to do after this lesson,
     with its timing if stated. Work only described as remaining, unfinished, left over or still to complete is not
     homework unless the tutor set it. If ${record} does not clearly show the tutor setting homework, return an empty
     string. Never repeat or restate the homework in topics, performance or improvement." (${record} = "the summary" /
     "the transcript".)
   - New rule 11 (summary mode): "11. Who did what: in the summary the student is always [STUDENT_1] and the tutor
     [TUTOR]. Any other name belongs to someone else — another student, a family member, a friend, or a person or
     character in the lesson material — never to [STUDENT_1], even when the summary seems to be about them. Never
     give [STUDENT_1] anything the summary says [TUTOR] or another named person did, said, finished or did not
     finish."
   - New rule 11 (transcript mode): "11. Who did what: only the lines labelled STUDENT are [STUDENT_1]'s own words
     and work; the lines labelled TUTOR are the tutor's. Anyone named in the lesson who is clearly not the student —
     another student, a family member, a friend, or a person or character in the lesson material — is never
     [STUDENT_1]: never give [STUDENT_1] what is said about them." Today's transcript rules 11 ("covered, not
     mastered") and 12 (Thai-script names) become 12 and 13, text unchanged.
   - FEEDBACK_JSON_SCHEMA homework description → "Only homework the tutor clearly set for after this lesson, with
     timing; empty string if none or unclear."
   - New exported `otherPeopleNamed(redactedSummary, studentFullName)` — summary mode only: capitalised words
     directly followed (optionally with also/only/just/then/still in between) by a person verb (said, says,
     mentioned, asked, answered, told, explained, noted, completed, finished, did, does, worked, struggled, solved,
     wrote, got, scored, submitted, had, has, was, is); drop a stop-list (The, They, He, She, It, We, I, You, This,
     That, Students, Student, Mr, Mrs, Ms, Miss, Teacher, Tutor, Parent, Mother, Father, Mum, Dad, days/months, etc.),
     words from the class details, the Soniox term list, and names that start with the student's first name or
     nickname (e.g. "Timothy" when the student is Tim); dedupe; cap 8. When non-empty, `buildFeedbackMessages` inserts
     a line before the evidence: "Other people named in the summary (never [STUDENT_1]): <name>, …". It is a hint,
     never a gate.
2. `judge.ts` (JUDGE_PROMPT_VERSION 3 → 4). System prompt (summary; the transcript variant swaps in the transcript
   wording and adds the STUDENT-label sentence and the "only shows the tutor explaining" sentence):
   ```
   You check a tutor's post-class feedback against an automatic summary of the same lesson.
   The student's and the tutor's names are replaced by [STUDENT_1] and [TUTOR]; that is expected. Any other name in the summary is someone else, never [STUDENT_1].
   [transcript: Only lines labelled STUDENT are the student's own words; anyone named in the lesson who is clearly not the student is someone else, never [STUDENT_1].]
   The class details come from the school's system and are true: naming the programme, exam or subject they give is supported.
   List every problem of these three kinds, quoting the feedback's own words:
   - unsupported: a factual claim about THIS lesson — topics, what the student did or got wrong, scores, materials, dates — that the {evidence} does not state or clearly imply. [transcript: Claiming the student understood or solved something the transcript only shows the tutor explaining is unsupported.]
   - misattributed: something the feedback says [STUDENT_1] did, said, finished, got wrong or did not finish, when the {evidence} says it about [TUTOR] or about another person.
   - homeworkNotSet: homework, a task or a due date the feedback says was set — everything under "Homework and due date", and any such statement in another field — unless the {evidence} clearly shows the tutor setting it for [STUDENT_1] to do after this lesson. Work only described as remaining, unfinished or still to complete was not set.
   General advice, encouragement and suggested practice (including practice before the next lesson) are fine and must not be listed, unless they are presented as homework the tutor set.
   faithful is true only when all three lists are empty.
   ```
   - Judge JSON schema: required `faithful` (boolean), `unsupported`, `misattributed`, `homeworkNotSet` (string
     arrays); strict zod parse — a missing list = unparseable (fail closed); `faithful` forced false if any list is
     non-empty.
   - The judge user message also gets the same "Other people named in the summary (never [STUDENT_1]): …" hint line
     when non-empty (summary mode).
   - New exported `judgeProblems(verdict)`: unsupported as-is, misattributed prefixed "wrong person: ",
     homeworkNotSet prefixed "homework not set: ". Old stored verdicts (`{faithful, unsupported}`) must still read
     fine where they are displayed (dashboard).
   - Keep: the judge is never given style guides or examples.
3. `pipeline.ts`: held reasons and call records use `judgeProblems`; pass the hint to the writer and judge message
   builders; transcripts still GLM only.
4. `config.ts`: widen the judge effort type to "max" | "high" | "medium" and set the judge to "high" (OpenRouter
   accepts it). Writer effort unchanged.
5. `dashboard.ts` (lib): `judgeUnsupported` (or equivalent) shows `judgeProblems` for new verdicts, `unsupported` for
   old ones.
6. Docs: `docs/features/feedback-autowriter.md` — a short "Writer and judge v4 (30 Sep)" subsection describing the
   new rules, the hint, the three judge lists and effort high.
