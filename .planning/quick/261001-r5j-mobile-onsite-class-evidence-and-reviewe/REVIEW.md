## Activation blockers and honest limits

- Keep the feature disabled until the additive schema, existing private Blob store/token, Soniox and OpenRouter capabilities, approved spend, and functioning independently enabled `CLASS_CAPTURE_RETENTION_ENABLED` worker are verified. Then separately authorize `ENABLE_CLASS_CAPTURE` and `CLASS_CAPTURE_PROCESSING_APPROVED`. Missing local values do not prove hosted services are disconnected.
- Verify real iOS/Android foreground recording, permission denial, lock/background/interruption, local recovery, lost network, large private upload, and private playback/seek. Browser mocks do not prove these device/provider behaviors; background recording is not promised.
- Validate provider/model English/Thai/mixed-language results, costs, deletion outcomes, and hosted scope freshness with authorized synthetic fixtures. Do not infer written/silent work or mastery from absent audio evidence. Worksheet images remain tutor-reviewed evidence, not model-interpreted work.
- The Soniox orphan scan is bounded to five pages per resource type and a deletion/time budget. Incomplete scans retain uncertainty and report failure; this is safe blocking behavior, not guaranteed eventual cleanup of a busy shared provider project. Confirm project load is traversable or add durable pagination before activation. Successful cleanup visit counts do not prove all copies have been purged.
- None of these unverified activation prerequisites prevents delivery of a disabled draft PR with screenshots, acceptance evidence, cost notes, and disclosed limits. They do prevent claiming production readiness or successful live transcription/deletion.
