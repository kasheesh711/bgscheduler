# Quick 260929-vj4: STUDENT feedback events must not prove tutor on-time submission

## Problem

`deriveEventTimingEvidence` (policy.ts) treats every non-auto
`SessionFeedbackSubmittedEvent` as proof the tutor submitted. A STUDENT actor
filling in their own session-feedback form therefore proves the tutor "on
time" and becomes `tutorSubmittedAt`. Prod (read-only SQL, 2026-09-29): 38
sessions are `on_time` only because of a STUDENT event; 300 more carry a
student instant as `tutorSubmittedAt`. The autowriter already excludes
students (`feedback-autowriter/submit.ts` `classifySubmitEvents`).

## Rule (D-EVT-05)

A qualifying event is non-auto AND not STUDENT. D-EVT-04 (staff account role
is not an authorship gate) is unchanged for TEACHER / ADMIN / OWNER / unknown.
