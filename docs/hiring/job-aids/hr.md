# Job aid: HR (people@)

One page. Open https://bgscheduler.vercel.app/hiring. The Overview opens first.

## Task 1. Who do I call next, and what is the phone number?
1. Click the **2 Calls** tab.
2. Read the first row. Booked calls come first, by time. Then the highest SEEK score.
3. Read the **Phone** column. Click the number to call.
4. After the call, click **Good call**, **No answer** or **Bad call** on that row.
   **Bad call** stops the candidate. A box asks "Stop <name>? Nothing is sent to them, and they leave every queue." Click **OK** only if you are sure.
5. If the list says "Calls are off", tell Kevin. He sets the HR call score in **Rules and setup**.

## Task 2. Which visits are today, or next?
1. Click the **Visit day** tab.
2. Click **Run sheet**.
3. Read the rows. Today is first. Each row shows the time, candidate, subject, head, paper and room.
4. To see old visits, tick **Show past visits (last 8 weeks)**.

## Task 3. What stage is a candidate at?
1. Click the **Overview** tab.
2. Find the table under the meters, above the charts.
3. Type the name in the search box marked "Search name, email or stage".
4. Read the **Stage** column.
5. Click the name to see the full record.

> **What "Needs review" means**
> The app does not have the data to show this field. It does not guess. The words after "Needs review:" give the reason. Do not use the visit until a person fixes the gap.

## Who to tell

| Gap on the row | Tell |
|---|---|
| Needs review: no head takes the subject, or 2 or more heads take it | Kevin (he owns the head roster in **Rules and setup**) |
| Needs review: no question paper | Kevin (he owns the paper library) |
| Needs review: no room is booked | Kevin. Rooms are not stored yet, so every visit shows this. Agree the room in person. |
| No pass mark | Kevin (pass marks are empty today) |

Messages on **Visit day → Message previews** are previews only. The app sends nothing from there. Copy the text by hand if you must send it.
