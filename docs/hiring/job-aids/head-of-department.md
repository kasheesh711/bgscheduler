# Job aid: Head of department

One page. Open https://bgscheduler.vercel.app/hiring. The Overview opens first.

## Task 1. When is the next mock for my subject, and with whom?
1. Click the **Visit day** tab.
2. Click **Heads: upcoming mocks**.
3. Find the block with your name. It lists your subjects.
4. Read the first row: time, candidate, subject.
5. If you have no upcoming visit, the table says "No upcoming mocks or paper visits." and **Recent visits (last 8 weeks)** shows under it.
6. "You run the mock" means you take the mock. "Paper only: no mock" means the candidate sits the paper only.

## Task 2. Which mocks do I have this week?
1. Click the **4 Mock and paper** tab.
2. Click **List**.
3. In **Head**, select your name.
4. In **Date**, select **This week**.
5. Read the rows. "No visits match these filters." means you have no mock this week.
6. Limit: the **Head** filter shows only mocks booked with you. Paper-only visits in your subject do not show here. To see them, use **Visit day → Heads: upcoming mocks**.

## Task 3. Where is the paper result after the visit?
1. Click the **4 Mock and paper** tab.
2. In **Head**, select **All**. Past results have no head, so a name in **Head** hides them.
3. In **Date**, select **Past**.
4. If the candidate is not in the list, click **Past results**.
5. Click the candidate's name.
6. Read the **Paper: <your subject>** section in the panel on the right.

> **What "Needs review" means**
> The app does not have the data to show this field. It does not guess. The words after "Needs review:" give the reason.

## Who to tell

| Gap | Tell |
|---|---|
| Needs review: no head, or 2 or more heads, for a subject | Kevin (head roster in **Rules and setup**) |
| Needs review: no question paper for your subject | Kevin (paper library). Send him the paper and mark scheme. |
| No pass mark / Pass mark missing | Kevin (pass marks are empty today) |
| No scan after the visit | Kevin (no owner for the scan upload yet) |
| Needs review: no room is booked | Kevin. Rooms are not stored yet. |

When messages are on, you get a LINE message when a mock is booked, moved or cancelled. The texts on **Visit day → Message previews** are previews only. The app does not send them.
