# 10-minute timed task test (HR, one head, Aya)

Use this test to confirm the new Hiring screens with the real users. Run it after PR #310 is on a test or production deployment. One person runs the test, and one person times it.

## Before the test (Kevin, 2 minutes)

1. Book or pick one visit for tomorrow that has a head and a paper. Check that one visit has a "Needs review" gap (for example English Language, which has no paper).
2. Print the job aid for each person (Kevin does tasks 9 to 11 on screen, no aid): `hr.md`, `head-of-department.md` and `admin-aya-thea.md`.
3. Give each person the printed aid. Do not show them the screen first.

## Rules

- Each person uses only their printed aid and the Hiring page.
- The timer starts when you read the task aloud. It stops when the person says the answer.
- Write down the time, the number of clicks and the answer. Do not help. If the person asks for help, write "help" and give the answer.
- A pass means the correct answer within the target time.

## Tasks

| # | Person | Task (read aloud) | Correct answer comes from | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR | "Who do you call next, and what is the phone number?" | 2 Calls, row 1 | 15 s, 3 clicks |
| 2 | HR | "Which visits are tomorrow? Give the time and subject." | Visit day → Run sheet | 30 s |
| 3 | HR | "What stage is <candidate name> in, and how many days?" | Overview table, Stage and Days in stage | 30 s |
| 4 | Head | "When is your next mock, and with whom?" | Visit day → Heads: upcoming mocks | 30 s |
| 5 | Head | "Which visits in your subjects are there this week?" | Visit day → Heads: upcoming mocks | 45 s |
| 6 | Aya | "Which paper do you print for each visit tomorrow?" | Visit day → Aya: print list, Paper column | 45 s |
| 7 | Aya | "Which visit has a problem, and whom do you tell?" | "Needs review" rows + the aid's Who to tell table | 45 s |
| 8 | Aya | "Which room is each visit in?" | Room column: "Needs review: no room is booked" (expected today) | 30 s |
| 9 | Kevin | "What share of candidates are hired so far?" | Overview → conversion meters | 30 s |
| 10 | Kevin | "Which candidates are stuck, and in which stage?" | Pipeline → stuck count, then the stuck list | 30 s |
| 11 | Kevin | "Which stage holds a candidate longest?" | Overview → time in stage | 30 s |

Total: about 7 minutes of tasks (the targets add up to 6 minutes 15 seconds) plus 3 minutes of set-up and notes, so the test fits 10 minutes.
