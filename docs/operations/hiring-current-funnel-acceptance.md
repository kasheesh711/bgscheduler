# Current hiring funnel acceptance rubric

Open **Hiring → Current funnel** with a controlled hiring account. Use synthetic applicants, an interviewer account, a restricted account, and a controlled recipient inbox. Start each task from the staff workspace. Record the browser, screen size, actions, expected result and observed result. Each row earns 10 points when the reviewer observes every result in that row. An incomplete or untested row earns zero. Release target: **100/100**.

| Points | Task | Pass condition |
|---:|---|---|
| 10 | Prioritize a candidate | Find the longest wait and the next action. Identify time in stage and time since last contact. |
| 10 | Verify the funnel | Recalculate a stage’s current and completed means from known dates and sample counts. Confirm that an unknown start stays out of the mean. Confirm that stage area equals its share of all active candidates and that zero-count stages remain selectable. In Sankey, follow SEEK → Subjects → Prescreen → Test → Human review → Interview → Final review → Offer letter. Match the SEEK total to unique people with a SEEK application ID. Match the initial score exits, current holds, and returns to the recorded decisions. Each score count opens the same people. A threshold change must preserve the recorded cutoff. Select a subject ribbon between two steps and match the names to the queue. Direct passes bypass Human review. An open head check ends at Human review. A head-approved paper can continue to Interview. A recorded waiver bypasses Test. A sent offer and a confirmed signature each require their own evidence. Confirm that a multi-subject candidate contributes one total unit of width and that a candidate with no subject appears under Subject unknown. |
| 10 | Use the queue | Find an older active candidate and an outstanding contract. Filter by stage, open the right record and return to the same list position. |
| 10 | Open a résumé | Open the correct original file with one activation. Identify a missing file from the row. |
| 10 | Escalate a contact | Identify an email-only case. Open its stored phone number, record an outcome and follow-up date, and verify both after refresh. |
| 10 | Move a candidate | Advance an eligible candidate by drag and by keyboard or touch. Observe the reason an ineligible move is blocked. |
| 10 | Waive a paper | Incomplete pre-screen blocks assessment and old booking links. A restricted account cannot waive a paper. A full administrator can record a waiver and carry the candidate through mock teaching to final review with the waiver reason and actual mock score. |
| 10 | Verify an invitation | Inspect the actual candidate message in the controlled inbox. Verify the From address, HTML, candidate name, next step and working link. Repeating the action yields one invitation. |
| 10 | Cancel a visit | Cancel a whole future visit and the remaining mock after a completed paper. Verify the affected appointments, one confirmation per visit, freed booking capacity and preserved paper result. |
| 10 | Operate the workspace | On desktop Safari and a phone-sized screen, see the queue on first load. Open a résumé or dialer in one activation. Move and cancel with one selection and one confirmation plus required input. A stale confirmation detects a concurrent change. |

Application screening covers incoming applications and legacy ATS holds. An audited SEEK import dates entry into BeGifted screening. Use the recorded intake time for that wait. Pre-screen covers submitted answers, including responses on hold. HR call requires a sent HR call invitation or a recorded booking. Final review follows mock teaching.

For dates, use Bangkok time. The current average includes known waits among candidates still in that stage. The completed average includes dated stays that ended in the last 84 days. Each active candidate appears once in the current queue. The Sankey includes all recorded SEEK candidates. Each person enters once, even with multiple SEEK applications. The Subjects column groups everyone outside the current score hold. Use assigned subjects first, then the subjects marked relevant in the recorded ATS assessment. Use Subject unknown when both sources are empty. The initial-filter count includes people who later returned. A recorded return and a current hold are separate outcomes. The paper review uses the existing subject-head request and decision records. Select Human review to see waiting, passed, and rejected cases. A milestone counts each person once. Ribbon widths share a multi-subject candidate equally. The header reports confirmed SEEK signatures separately from sent offers. A recorded waiver connects Prescreen directly to Interview.

Record live Gmail delivery and Ek’s actual inbox sign-in separately from local acceptance. A simulated transport can prove the generated MIME and deduplication behavior.

## Visual review

Award 20 points for each observed result. Check the full SEEK intake and a single-subject view on desktop and a phone-sized screen. Use a masked read-only copy of real records to check subject density and rare paths.

| Points | Check | Required result |
|---:|---|---|
| 20 | Chart prominence | The Sankey opens first. The chart and first queue record are visible together. |
| 20 | Legibility | Read all eight step labels, source totals and score exits without overlap. Identify each subject from its color and label or picker. |
| 20 | Paths | Trace the intake cutoff, direct test pass, subject-head review and paper waiver from evidence. Zero totals remain visible. |
| 20 | Interaction | Select a subject, ribbon, or cutoff count. Match the queue and milestone totals to the selection. Clear it to restore the active queue. |
| 20 | Phone layout | Use the subject picker, intake outcomes and queue at 390 × 844 without horizontal page scrolling. |
